Privacy Policy for CyBus

Effective date: 05 05, 2025

CyBus respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application.

### 1. Information We Collect
- **Usage data**: interactions with the app (screens visited, session length).  
- **Advertising identifiers**: Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) and other identifiers, only if you grant permission via App Tracking Transparency (ATT).  

### 2. How We Use Information
- To provide and improve app functionality.  
- To measure performance and usage statistics.  
- To show **personalized ads (if you consent via ATT)** or **non-personalized ads** via Google AdMob if you decline tracking.  

### 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
Our app uses **Google AdMob** to display ads.  
- If you allow tracking via ATT, AdMob may use your advertising identifier (IDFA) to serve personalized ads.  
- If you deny tracking, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized.  
- For more details, see [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).  

### 4. Diagnostic Data
We may collect anonymous diagnostic information for improving app performance and stability. This data does not include personal identifiers and is used solely for debugging purposes.  

### 5. Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal data. Limited data may be shared with service providers (such as Google AdMob) to serve ads and analytics.  

### 6. User Choices
- **App Tracking Transparency (ATT):** You can allow or deny tracking in the iOS system prompt, and change your choice later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.  
- **Ads personalization:** You can manage your ad preferences in your Google account: [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).  

### 7. Data Retention
We retain collected data only as long as necessary to provide the app and comply with legal obligations.
